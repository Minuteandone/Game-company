Small, reversible gameplay experiment

Forked Spectrum

One white beam reaches a fixed prism and splits into two rays. Route cyan to A and amber to B by rotating mirrors. Both receivers must connect at once.

After splitter P, cyan travels north and amber travels south. Mirror tiles are buttons: Tab moves between them, Enter or Space rotates. A is the cyan goal and B the amber goal.

Experimental outcome

Tracing rays…

Cyan → A Not connected

Amber → B Not connected

Rotations: 0

No timer, sound, tracking, save data, scores, or external dependencies. This does not replace the original Prism Relay game.

What this tests: Does branching light toward two receivers require different thinking from a single-route puzzle? A playable rule is not proof of player enjoyment. The baseline Prism Relay remains unchanged.
