Coupled Garden

One move turns an entire row or column of paths. Connect the fixed star in the upper-left to the flower in the lower-right without rotating individual tiles.

One little network, eight coupled controls

Choose ↻ R1–R4 to rotate every movable pipe in a row, or ↻ C1–C4 for a column. Each button turns its group 90° clockwise; star and flower stay fixed. Paths glow only when their neighbors face each other. The puzzle is solvable in four moves.

Unlike the original Signal Garden, there are no single-tile turns or exact hints. Each choice changes multiple connections.

Tiny hypothesis test

Does a move feel consequential?

Observe how several pipes change when you make one move. A path only carries light across mutually facing arms.
